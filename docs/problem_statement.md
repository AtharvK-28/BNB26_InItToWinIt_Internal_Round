# CreatorAi: AI-Powered Creator Operating Platform

Source: problem statement supplied by the founder. Wording preserved; line wrapping and heading formatting normalized.

## Backstory

The creator economy is growing rapidly, but content creation remains fragmented across tools for scripting, asset storage, editing, scheduling, and publishing. This leads to scattered assets, repeated work, context switching, and inefficient content production.

## Problem Statement

Develop CreatorAi, an AI-powered content operations platform that unifies the creator workflow from idea and script to recording, editing, repurposing, and publishing. The system should understand both textual and visual content, intelligently organize creator assets, and automate repetitive production tasks.

The platform must be capable of taking a script and corresponding raw footage, identifying relevant sections, generating short-form clips, creating hooks and supporting content, and adapting the resulting content for different platforms. AI-generated edits should remain editable so that creators retain control over the final output.

## Key Features

- **Asset Management:** Centralized management of videos, images, audio, and other creator assets.
- **AI Script & Hook Generation:** Generate hooks, scripts, and supporting content.
- **Script-to-Video Understanding:** Understand the relationship between scripts and corresponding footage.
- **Automated Clip Generation:** Identify relevant sections of long-form footage and generate short-form clips.
- **AI-Assisted Editing:** Apply AI-generated edits while keeping the resulting content editable.
- **Multi-Platform Adaptation:** Adapt content for different platforms and formats.
- **Content Workflow:** Manage the content lifecycle from idea to publishing.
- **Creator Intelligence:** Provide insights into content performance and production patterns.
